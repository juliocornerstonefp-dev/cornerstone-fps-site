Accumulation Is Not the Same as Distribution

The financial industry has spent decades teaching people how to save. And for the most part, it worked. The problem is that the skills that build a nest egg are not the same skills that turn it into income.

Accumulation is about growth, diversification, and time in the market. Distribution is about sequencing, tax efficiency, guaranteed floors, and longevity risk. They require fundamentally different strategies.

Most advisors are trained in accumulation. They manage portfolios. They optimize for returns. But when a client walks in and says, "I'm retiring next year — now what?" — they don't always have a clear answer.

That's where we start. Every plan we build answers the three questions that matter most in retirement:

1. How much guaranteed income can I count on every month?
2. How long will my money last under different scenarios?
3. What happens if the market drops 30% in my first year of retirement?

If your current plan can't answer those questions clearly, we should talk.
